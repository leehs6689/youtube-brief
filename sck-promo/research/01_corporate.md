## ⚠️ KEY FRAMING ISSUES (read first)

1. **The site is NOT in Songdo or Bupyeong.** It is on **Yeongjong Island (영종도), Incheon Jung-gu (중구), in the Incheon Airport Free Trade Zone (인천공항 자유무역지역), Unseo-dong (운서동).**
2. **"STATS ChipPAC Korea" in Korea is TWO legal entities on neighbouring lots:**
   - **(유)스태츠칩팩코리아 / STATS ChipPAC Korea Ltd. (SCK)** — the legacy entity descended from Hyundai Electronics' assembly division. Address: 191 Jayumuyeok-ro.
   - **제이셋스태츠칩팩코리아(유) / JCET STATS ChipPAC Korea Ltd. (JSCK)** — a new entity JCET set up after the acquisition (incorporated 2015-12-24). Address: 299 Jayumuyeok-ro.
   - Press reports often merge the two (for example the USD 2.8B revenue and 5,000-employee figures). Decide which entity, or the combined "Korea operation", the video is about.
3. Both entities are **유한회사 (limited company)**, not 주식회사. One 2014 government release calls it "(주)" in one excerpt and "(유)" in its title, so there is an inconsistency there.
