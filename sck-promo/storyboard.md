# Storyboard — STATS ChipPAC Korea 60s (16:9, 60fps, 120 BPM)

**Concept.** The film follows one idea: a chip is complete only once it connects to the world.

**Signature motif.** The magenta dot on the "i" of the logo stands for a solder bump, the point of connection. It appears in every scene and lands back on the logo at the end.

**Brand tokens.** Taken from the company PPT theme and logo:
- Colors: Navy #0000A0, Blue #004BFF, Sky #0098FF, Cyan #00D6FF, Magenta #CC25B8, Pink #FF4ABD
- Background: #03051A
- Font: Pretendard

**Transitions.** Three kinds are used:
- Match cut on the magenta dot
- Brand-gradient wipe
- Scale punch

**Captions.** The narration text sits at the bottom of the frame. Each paragraph is split into phrases, and each phrase is timed by its share of the characters.

**Narration.** AI narration generated with the vidIQ/ElevenLabs "Brian" voice. Internal pauses are capped at 0.2 s. Korean runs at ×1.11–1.14 and English at ×1.0–1.13 (pitch preserved), so both languages share one set of cuts.
